For Universities

Partner with UKABCS as a University

UKABCS works with universities as a co-funding partner. We cover part of the cost, and your institution or students cover only the essential part.

Request a partnership
What is the University Partner Programme?

One agreement that opens many benefits

The UKABCS University Partner Programme is a single, general cooperation protocol between your university and UKABCS. Once it is signed, your students, researchers and faculty can join UKABCS programmes without negotiating a new contract each time.

Through the programme, UKABCS brings together the support of its partner companies, partner universities and volunteer experts: graduation project support, training, compute and AI tools, mobility exchange, publication and conference support, and startup support.

  • What it coversTen areas of cooperation, added as you need them
  • How it is fundedShared cost: in-kind, subsidised or cash support from UKABCS
How our support works

A co-funded partnership, not only a grant

Every UKABCS programme shares the cost. UKABCS supports part through donated, subsidised or cash support. The university or student pays only the essential costs, and every budget and fee is published.

In-kind

Services donated to UKABCS by partner companies, such as compute, research platforms, CRM licences and training, provided to your students and researchers at no charge.

Subsidised

Services offered well below market price, such as GPU hours and AI usage, with discounts set by your agreement tier.

Cash

Direct, milestone-based support where a programme provides it, such as mobility pocket money, PI allowances or startup marketing funds.

Agreements

Types of cooperation agreement

Most universities start with a Framework MoU and add specific agreements as they join programmes.

Framework MoU

The starting agreement that opens all ten areas of cooperation and sets the general rights and obligations of both parties.

Co-funded Grant Agreement

Sets each party’s share of a specific grant, for example the Digital Campus Transformation Grant (60% UKABCS, 40% university).

Compute & AI Access Agreement

Gives your students and researchers subsidised GPU hours and AI research tools at a tiered discount: Bronze 50%, Silver 60% or Gold 70%.

Mobility Agreement

Joins your university to the UKABCS exchange network. Each university signs with UKABCS directly; UKABCS coordinates placements with the other partner universities.

Tool Contribution Agreement

Covers research tools built by your researchers or graduation teams for the UKABCS AI research platform: credit, intellectual property and free public access.

Industry In-kind Donation Agreement

For companies donating services to UKABCS that are then provided to partner universities and researchers.

Faculty Development Agreement

Structured training programmes for faculty members in research tools, AI and computational methods.

Rights & obligations

Know what you give and what you get

Each agreement sets out, before signing, what the university and its students receive and what they commit to.

University — receives

  • Access to the programmes named in its agreement
  • In-kind and subsidised services for its students and researchers
  • Priority in UKABCS grant calls
  • Coordination with UKABCS partner universities and companies
  • Clear, published budgets and administration fees

University — commits to

  • Its agreed co-funding share, paid directly to implementing companies under contract
  • Appointing a responsible PI or focal point
  • Following programme rules, timelines and reporting
  • Nominating eligible students fairly and transparently
  • Hosting incoming exchange participants where it joins the mobility network

Student — receives

  • The support package of the programme they are selected for
  • Access to donated tools, platforms and compute
  • Support letters and coordination where applicable
  • Certificates and recognition on completion

Student — commits to

  • Paying any costs the programme states are their own (for example flights, visa or a declared administration fee)
  • Completing the programme requirements and timelines
  • Following the host institution’s rules and UKABCS policies
  • Submitting the required reports and outputs
Process

From request to results

  1. 1

    Apply

    Submit a partnership request describing your institution and the areas you are interested in.

  2. 2

    Review

    UKABCS reviews eligibility and proposes the agreements and tier that fit your needs.

  3. 3

    Sign

    Your university signs directly with UKABCS. Rights and obligations are set out before any work starts.

  4. 4

    Implement

    Programmes run with milestones, and payments are made against evidence.

  5. 5

    Report

    Both parties report on outcomes, and results are published in line with our transparency policies.
