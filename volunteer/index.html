Get Involved

Volunteer with UKABCS

Share your knowledge, skills and time to support researchers, students and our scientific community.

Explore volunteering opportunities
Make a difference

Give your expertise a wider impact

Volunteers help bring people, ideas and opportunities together. Whether you guide a research team, teach a workshop, mentor learners or support the association’s work, your contribution can help our community grow.

Explore the opportunities below and choose the role that fits your experience and interests.

Academic supervision

Volunteer Academic Supervisor

Guide the next generation of researchers

Share your research experience with a student team working on a graduation or research project. Help students develop their ideas, navigate research challenges and build confidence in their scientific work.

For university faculty members and researchers with relevant research or supervision experience.

  • Online
  • 3 months
  • Approximately 2 hours per week
  • Voluntary and unpaid
Apply as an Academic Supervisor →
Teaching & workshops

Volunteer Workshop Instructor

Share your knowledge through workshops

Bring your expertise to learners through online scientific, technical or professional workshops. Choose a topic with the UKABCS team and help participants develop practical knowledge and skills.

For academics, researchers, industry professionals and experienced instructors.

  • Online
  • Weekly participation by agreement
  • Workshops may run for 1–3 days
  • Voluntary and unpaid
Apply as a Workshop Instructor →
Mentorship

Volunteer Mentor

Help others grow with your experience

Support groups of students and early-career researchers with guidance in research, academic development, projects or professional growth. Share perspectives that help participants take their next steps.

For academics, researchers, industry professionals and experienced specialists.

  • Online group sessions
  • 2-month programme
  • Schedule agreed with UKABCS
  • Voluntary and unpaid
Apply as a Volunteer Mentor →
Organisation & operations

Association & Operations Volunteer

Support the work behind our community

Contribute your organisational and administrative skills to support the association’s activities. Volunteers can help with coordination, events and the day-to-day work that brings our community together.

For people who would like to contribute their time and organisational skills to the association.

  • Responsibilities and availability agreed with UKABCS
  • Voluntary and unpaid

UKABCS

UK Organisation for Biotechnology and Computational Science (UKABCS) is a UK-registered non-profit academic society dedicated to peer-reviewed research, transparent grant funding, and open-access scientific education.
