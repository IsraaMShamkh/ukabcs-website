About the programme

What is UKABCS Copyright Registration for Innovation?

Researchers create valuable work every day: new methods, software, datasets, designs and inventions. In most countries this work is protected by copyright from the moment it is created. The difficulty comes later, when someone needs to prove what they created and when.

UKABCS Copyright Registration for Innovation solves this. You register your work with UKABCS, an independent UK-registered academic organisation. We keep it on record with its date and issue a Certificate of Copyright & Innovation Registration with a unique serial number that anyone you choose can verify online.

From there, UKABCS helps you go further: show your work publicly, find out how new it is, prepare it for patent filing, and turn it into a product or a startup.

Which registration is right for me?

Choose Confidential if you might file a patent, if your work is unpublished, or if you simply want private proof of authorship. Choose Public Register if you want your work to be seen and credited, for example a software tool, a method or a finished project. In both cases the full work itself is never published.

Feature article, novelty report and technical dossier

Once your work is registered, these services help you take the next step. A feature article makes you and your work known. A novelty report tells you how new your idea is. A technical dossier prepares your invention for formal patent filing.

Who it is for: Researchers who want their work and expertise to be known by universities, industry, funders and the public.

Preliminary Novelty Report

£1,000 per idea

What it is: A written report that shows what already exists close to your idea, so you know how new it is before you invest more time or money.

Who it is for: Researchers and students who are thinking of filing a patent, starting a company or publishing a new method.

  • A recommended next step for your work

Before you register

Important information

Please read these points so you know exactly what the certificate means and how to protect your work in the best way.

What the certificate records

The certificate records that the named author registered the named work with UKABCS on the stated date. It is independent, dated evidence of the work, its authorship and its content.

Patents and official registrations

A UKABCS copyright certificate is a private registration record. It is not a patent and not a government registration. Patents, trade marks and registered designs are granted only by national or regional intellectual property offices.

If you plan to file a patent

Making an invention public before filing can affect whether it can be patented. Choose the confidential registration and file first. Nothing is published without your written approval.

Work created at a university

Many universities have their own intellectual property policy. Please check it before registering work created at your institution, and add your institution’s approval where it applies.

Our role

UKABCS provides registration, technical, documentation and research support. Formal patent filing and legal advice are provided by licensed patent attorneys or your institution’s technology transfer office.

Confidentiality

Your work and everything you share with us is kept confidential and used only to deliver the service you asked for.

FAQ

Questions about copyright registration

What is UKABCS copyright registration?

It is a dated record of your work held by an independent organisation. You send your work to UKABCS, we keep it on record with the date we received it, and you receive a Certificate of Copyright & Innovation Registration with a unique serial number. If anyone later questions who created the work or when, the registration is independent evidence.

Why register my work if copyright already protects it?

In most countries copyright belongs to the author automatically. The difficulty is proving what you created and when. A dated registration with an independent body gives you that proof.

Who can use these services?

Researchers, faculty members, postgraduate and undergraduate students, research teams and institutions in any country. You do not need to be a UKABCS member.

What kinds of work can I register?

Research ideas and methods, software and source code, datasets, algorithms, designs, protocols, dashboards, manuscripts, theses and project reports.

Confidential or public register: which should I choose?

Choose confidential if you may file a patent or want to keep the work private. Choose the public register if you want your work to be seen, credited and cited. In both, the full work stays confidential; the public register shows only the summary you approve.

How does someone check that a certificate is genuine?

Every certificate has a serial number. Enter it on the UKABCS verification page (ukabcs.co.uk/verify) to see the certificate exactly as UKABCS issued it.

Can I move from confidential to the public register later?

Yes. Contact us and we will upgrade your registration. You pay the difference between the two packages for the same term.

Can a team register one work?

Yes. One registration can name several authors from the same team, and all names appear on the certificate.

Is funding available?

Prototype development, startup mentoring and demo day support may be co-funded through UKABCS grant programmes, including InnoFund, Scientific Software Development and the Graduation Project Startup Grant, when a call is open.

Can my university partner with UKABCS on copyright registration?

Yes. Partner universities sign a cooperation agreement with UKABCS that opens copyright registration and these services to their staff and students.
