# UKABCS Digital Platform Context

UK Association for Biotechnology & Computational Science (UKABCS) web platform serving as an international non-profit portal for researcher memberships, peer-reviewed grant calls, funded projects showcase, and open-access scientific resources.

## Language

### Governance & Identity
**UKABCS**:
The UK Association for Biotechnology & Computational Science, a UK-registered non-profit academic society incorporated in 2025.
_Avoid_: Company, commercial agency, firm

**Member**:
An academic, student, professional, or institutional subscriber holding a recognized UKABCS membership tier.
_Avoid_: Customer, subscriber, user

### Programmes & Funding
**Grant Call**:
An official funding announcement with formal eligibility criteria, application windows, peer-review procedures, and governance oversight.
_Avoid_: Contest, competition, tender

**Funded Project**:
A peer-reviewed scientific initiative or graduation project formally awarded financial support, vouchers, or resources by UKABCS.
_Avoid_: Contract, freelance job, order

**Research Credit**:
In-kind computational resources, analytical service vouchers, or fee waivers donated by institutional and industry partners (e.g., Bioinformatics Gate, Exovion, HealOra).
_Avoid_: Promo code, cash prize, discount coupon

**ClientFlow Form**:
External secure CRM form hosted on `clientflow-crm.com` handling applicant data ingestion, reviews, and ticketing.
_Avoid_: Internal database form, direct backend form

### Architecture & Discovery
**GEO / Agentic Optimization**:
Generative Engine Optimization and AI agent discoverability implemented via JSON-LD schemas, semantic HTML, and `llms.txt` to enable autonomous research agents and LLMs to index and cite UKABCS.
_Avoid_: Basic keyword stuffing, old SEO tricks

**Design System**:
A minimalist, flat, high-end visual aesthetic inspired by Microsoft Fluent and Google Material 3 featuring clean typography, ample whitespace, and subtle ambient blur backdrops instead of heavy bordered cards.
_Avoid_: Boxy cards, template widgets, heavy drop shadows
