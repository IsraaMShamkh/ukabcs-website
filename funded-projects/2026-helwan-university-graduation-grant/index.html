2026 Award Graduation Project Capacity Building & Enhanced Experimental Support Grant Egypt 1 September 2026

A New Milestone for Emerging Researchers: UKABCS Congratulates Helwan University on Winning the 2026 Graduation Project Grant & Welcomes Them to the UKABCS Family!

Lead Institution: Helwan University (Egypt)

Empowering undergraduate researchers across Biotechnology, Biochemistry, and Biological Sciences with computational pipelines and practical chemical consumables.

Grantor: UK Organisation for Biotechnology and Computational Science (UKABCS)
Awarded Institution: Helwan University — Faculty of Science
Country: Arab Republic of Egypt
Main Principal Investigator (Main PI): Designated Faculty of Science Main PI / Institutional Representative
Article Type: Official Press Release & Congratulatory Website Article
Approval Date: September 2026

1. Official Announcement & Heartfelt Congratulations

The UK Organisation for Biotechnology and Computational Science (UKABCS) extends its warmest congratulations to Helwan University — Faculty of Science on winning the UKABCS Graduation Project Capacity Building & Enhanced Experimental Support Grant 2026.

This award recognizes the scientific excellence and innovation of Helwan University’s Faculty of Science, empowering undergraduate researchers to pioneer new frontiers in biological and computational sciences.

2. Student Capacity & Scale of Impact

The 2026 grant provides comprehensive training and experimental support for undergraduate researchers at Helwan University:

  • Awarded Research Teams: Full capacity support for participating graduation project teams across Biotechnology, Biochemistry, and Biological Sciences.
  • Participating Cohort: Capacity building and practical support for undergraduate students across the 3-year grant term (2026–2029).

3. Dual-Phase Capacity Building Framework

Participating student teams will undertake a specialized research journey governed by the UKABCS Dual-Phase Framework:

Phase I: Capacity Building & Computational Training

  • Full Platform Access: Provision of credentials to UKABCS digital tools, software pipelines, and bio-databases.
  • Specialized Training: Advanced coursework in Computational Biology, Structural Modeling, Virtual Screening, and AI in Life Sciences.

Phase II: Project Execution & Experimental Validation

  • Wet-Lab Validation: Practical experimental support to validate computational predictions within university laboratories.
  • Publication Mentorship: Academic guidance for drafting, refining, and publishing research papers in peer-reviewed international journals.

4. Enhanced Experimental Support & Chemical Cash Fund

To support practical laboratory synthesis and testing, UKABCS has introduced an enhanced experimental funding track:

  • Annual Chemical & Reagents Allocation: A dedicated cash funding enhancement of £3,000 GBP per year allocated exclusively for purchasing approved Kits & Reagents and laboratory consumables for winning project teams.
  • Total Experimental Funding: Reaches £9,000 GBP across the 3-year grant cycle.
  • Direct Supplier Disbursement: Procurement payments are disbursed directly by UKABCS to verified chemical suppliers via Lab Chem Flow.

5. Main PI Leadership & Governance

The designated Main Principal Investigator (Main PI) at Helwan University’s Faculty of Science leads the awarded grant. Under Clause 10.1 of the agreement, responsibilities include:

  • Scientific Review & Specification Approval: Evaluating and scientifically approving technical specifications for Kits & Reagents submitted by research teams.
  • Digital Management via Lab Chem Flow: Overseeing procurement requests, inventory checks, and chemical dispensing through Lab Chem Flow.
  • Resource Consolidation: Managing shared chemical utilization among research teams to maximize research efficiency.
  • Reporting & Publication Governance: Submitting Quarterly Progress Reports and Annual Institutional Reports to UKABCS while maintaining institutional attribution for Helwan University and UKABCS.

6. Welcome to the UKABCS Family!

The UK Organisation for Biotechnology and Computational Science (UKABCS) is delighted to officially welcome Helwan University (Faculty of Science), its faculty members, and student researchers into the global UKABCS Family.

We are immensely proud of this collaboration and look forward to celebrating your scientific breakthroughs. Welcome to your UKABCS Family!
