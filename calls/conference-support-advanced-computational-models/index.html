Publications & Conferences

UKABCS Support for the International Conference on Advanced Computational Models in Medicine and Biotechnology

50% reduction in official registration fees for accepted oral/poster presenters and delegates attending in London or virtually.

Call Status OPEN
Deadline 10 Dec 2025
Budget / Coverage 50% Registration Fee Reduction
Eligibility Open to both UKABCS Members and Non-Members with accepted abstracts or relevant research background.

Call Overview

The UK Association for Biotechnology & Computational Science (UKABCS) is pleased to sponsor registration subsidies for researchers presenting their scientific findings at the:

International Conference on Advanced Computational Models in Medicine and Biotechnology Location: London, United Kingdom (Hybrid: In-Person & Online Presentations Accepted) Application Deadline: 10 December 2025

Support Categories

  1. Category A: Presenting Authors (Oral / Poster):
    • 50% direct reduction on standard and early-bird registration fees.
    • Fast-track conference presentation slot.
    • Official conference certificate featuring UKABCS co-sponsorship endorsement.
  2. Category B: General Delegates & Early-Career Scholars:
    • 50% reduction on delegate registration to encourage capacity building and international academic networking.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Open to both UKABCS Members and Non-Members.
  • Non-members must submit a brief Statement of Purpose and academic CV explaining how their participation advances biotechnology and computational biology research.

Application Procedure

  1. Obtain your conference acceptance letter or submit your draft abstract.
  2. Complete the UKABCS General Support Application Form below.
  3. Attach your proof of submission/acceptance and current CV.
  4. Notifications of award are issued within 5 working days of application.
  • Apply Online: UKABCS General Support Portal
