# UKABCS Website Expansion Plan (Final, Consolidated)

_Last updated: 2026-10-19_

## 0. Core Principles

- **Currency:** All amounts across the site are in **GBP (£)**.
- **Model:** UKABCS moves from "grant-maker only" to **co-funded partner**. UKABCS supports part of the cost; the university or student pays only the essential part.
- **Three support instruments:**
  - **In-kind** — services donated by partner companies (compute, CRM, training).
  - **Subsidised** — services at reduced price (GPU hours, LLM usage).
  - **Cash** — direct payments (pocket money, PI allowance, marketing funds).
- **Contracts:** Each university signs **directly and separately with UKABCS**. UKABCS coordinates with the other partner universities. From day one the agreement states clearly what the university gives and gets, and what the student gives and gets.
- **Ebonex:** One partner among many — not the centre of the site. GPU/AI content lives under **Software & Development**.
- **Administration fees:** Always declared publicly and transparently (what they cover and why they cannot be waived).

## 3. New Page — Mobility Exchange (`/mobility-exchange`)

Replaces the current two-card `InternationalMobilitySection`.

- **How it works:** Each university signs individually with UKABCS; UKABCS coordinates placements across its partner university network.
- **Who pays what:**

| Party | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Student | Flights, visa, insurance |
| Host university | Accommodation (hosting), acceptance letter |
| UKABCS | Pocket money, coordination, support letters, access to compute tools |

- **Pocket money:** set **per host country** and stated in the host university's MoU; may vary per grant call.
- **Obligations** of sending university and student (rules and conditions).
- **Disclaimer:** visa decisions rest with the embassy, not UKABCS.
- Keep existing Outbound and Incoming application forms.
