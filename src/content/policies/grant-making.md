# UKABCS Grant-Making & Allocation Governance Policy

UKABCS awards competitive research and capacity building grants through a rigorous, independent double-blind peer-review evaluation system.

## 1. Principles of Grant Allocation
- **Merit & Quality:** Proposals are scored on technical feasibility, scientific rigor, and potential research impact.
- **Independence:** External reviewers evaluate applications without bias or commercial conflict of interest.
- **Transparency:** Clear evaluation metrics and feedback are provided to all applicant teams.
