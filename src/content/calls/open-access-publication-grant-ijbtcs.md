## Program Summary

The **UK Association for Biotechnology & Computational Science (UKABCS)** is committed to ensuring that financial barriers never impede the global dissemination of peer-reviewed research. 

Through our institutional publishing fund, we offer **full and partial Article Processing Charge (APC) waivers** exclusively for manuscripts submitted to and accepted by:

> **International Journal of Biotechnology & Computational Science (IJBTCS)**
> *Indexed in major scholarly indexes with immediate Gold Open Access (CC-BY).*

---

## Scope & Eligible Domains

Waivers are available for manuscripts reporting original research, comprehensive reviews, or novel computational pipelines in:

* **Biotechnology & Molecular Biology:** Recombinant technologies, fermentation, synthetic biology, and bioprocess engineering.
* **Computational Biology & Bioinformatics:** Structural bioinformatics, macromolecular simulation, molecular docking, and genomics.
* **Drug Discovery & Chemoinformatics:** Target identification, QSAR, pharmacophore modeling, and lead optimization.
* **Artificial Intelligence in Life Sciences:** Deep learning for protein design, AlphaFold workflows, and biomedical imaging.

---

## Coverage Options

* **Full Waiver (100% APC Coverage):** Awarded to primary authors from low-to-middle income countries, early-career researchers without institutional grants, and active UKABCS members with outstanding peer review scores.
* **Partial Waiver (50% APC Coverage):** Awarded on competitive merit to co-funded academic teams.

---

## Application Requirements

1. Official manuscript acceptance letter (or provisional acceptance subject to minor revisions) from IJBTCS.
2. Verified active UKABCS Membership ID.
3. Brief institutional confirmation that no dedicated open-access funds are available from the authors' host department.
