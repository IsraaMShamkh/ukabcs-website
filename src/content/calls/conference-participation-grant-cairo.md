## Program Summary

The **UK Association for Biotechnology & Computational Science (UKABCS)** is pleased to invite applications for the **Conference Participation Support Grant**, offered in conjunction with our academic partners at Cairo University Faculty of Science.

---

## Two Award Tracks

### Track A: Full Registration Coverage
* **What is covered:** 100% of the conference registration fee.
* **Benefits:** Full access to all keynote sessions, parallel oral tracks, poster exhibition, networking receptions, and published conference proceedings.

### Track B: Registration + Travel & Accommodation Subsidy
* **What is covered:** Full registration fee **plus up to $2,000 USD** towards verified travel, visa, and hotel expenses.
* **Eligible Applicants:** Primary presenting authors traveling internationally or from outside the host city, prioritized for early-career researchers, PhD fellows, and under-represented institutions.

---

## Key Deadlines & Timetable

* **Application Deadline:** **20 September 2026**
* **Conference Dates:** 10 – 12 October 2026
* **Venue:** Conference Hall, Faculty of Science, Cairo University, Giza, Egypt

---

## How to Apply

1. Ensure your abstract has been submitted to the scientific organizing committee.
2. Complete the online UKABCS Conference Registration Fee Funding Application.
3. Upload your accepted abstract notification, CV, and institutional affiliation proof.
