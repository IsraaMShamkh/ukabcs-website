## Call Overview

UKABCS is proud to announce co-sponsorship and financial subsidies for the upcoming flagship academic gathering:

> **Third International Conference on Computational Science and Biotechnology (ICCSB 2026)**
> *Location:* London, UK (Hybrid Attendance)
> *Application Deadline:* **15 March 2026**

---

## Award Highlights

* **50% Registration Fee Coverage:** Awarded directly against official conference invoice.
* **Publication Track:** Selected high-scoring papers presented at the conference are invited to submit expanded full manuscripts for open-access peer review in the *International Journal of Biotechnology & Computational Science (IJBTCS)* with priority fee waivers.
* **Global Visibility:** Present your research before leading PIs from top European and Middle Eastern computational biology labs.

---

## Who Should Apply?

* Academic scholars, postdocs, and university faculty.
* Master's and doctoral candidates with peer-reviewed oral or poster abstracts accepted by the conference committee.
* Verified undergraduate graduation project teams with computational pipelines.

---

Submit your application through the official UKABCS Grant Portal:
* **Online Application:** [UKABCS General Support Form](https://clientflow-crm.com/f/ukabcs/ukabcs-general-support/ukabcs-general-support-form)
* **Secretariat Support:** `support@ukabcs.co.uk`
