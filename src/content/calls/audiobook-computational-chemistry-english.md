## Overview

UKABCS, in collaboration with **EBO Bio Solution (UK)**, provides digital audiobook access to:

> **Essentials of Computational Chemistry: Theories and Models (English Edition)**

Narrated and curated by senior computational chemistry educators, this resource enables mobile and audio-first study of core quantum mechanics, molecular dynamics, and molecular modeling paradigms.

---

## Key Modules

1. **Fundamental Concepts of Molecular Mechanics:** Force fields, energy minimization algorithms, and conformational searching.
2. **Semi-Empirical & Ab Initio Electronic Structure:** MO theory, basis sets, polarization, and electron correlation methods.
3. **Density Functional Theory:** Modern hybrid functionals, dispersion corrections (D3/D4), and transition metal complexes.
4. **Condensed-Phase Simulations:** Periodic boundary conditions, NPT/NVT ensembles, and free energy calculations.
