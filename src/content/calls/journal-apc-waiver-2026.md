The **UKABCS Open-Access APC Waiver Grant** covers full manuscript processing fees for high-impact computational biology, bioinformatics, and chemoinformatics research.

### Key Guidelines
- Double-blind peer review required.
- Author must hold an active UKABCS membership.
- Applied directly upon paper acceptance in indexed partner journals.
