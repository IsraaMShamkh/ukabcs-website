In light of the profound transformations currently shaping global academia and research, it has become clear that the future of science increasingly depends on the integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and computational technologies across all disciplines. Research is no longer confined to traditional laboratories or costly experimentation; every researcher, university, and student must now acquire the computational skillset necessary to interpret results accurately, analyse data, and enhance scientific output.

Against this backdrop, the **UK Association for Biotechnology and Computational Science (UKABCS)** is proud to launch the next cycle of its **Graduation Project Capacity Building Grants**. This initiative enables universities and higher education institutions worldwide to support senior undergraduate students in executing high-impact, computationally driven graduation projects. It represents a strategic step toward empowering the next generation of researchers and cultivating an academic culture capable of competing on the global stage.

**Round 2 — Applications Open** | Apply by **1 November 2026**. Applications are open for two weeks.

## Application Rounds

- **Round 1 — Closed:** 15 June–15 July, extended to 30 July.
- **Round 2 — Open:** 18 October–1 November 2026 (two weeks).
- **Round 3 — Planned:** December 2026–January 2027. Exact dates will be announced.

### Round 2 — Call Modifications

Institutions may apply with **10–25 graduation project teams**. Institutions with **10–19 teams** are eligible for the programme without chemicals support. Institutions with **20–25 teams** are eligible for **£3,000 in chemicals support per awarded institution**, not per team. Phase II project implementation, administration and hosting are provided at zero cost to students. Completed projects may apply separately for the competitive [UKABCS Graduation Project Startup Grant](/news/ukabcs-graduation-project-startup-grant).

## Programme Structure — A Two-Phase Framework

In alignment with UKRI transparency, research integrity, and accountability standards, the Graduation Project Capacity Building Grant operates under a structured Two-Phase Framework:

### Phase I — Mandatory Preparatory Training Programmes (Non-Grant-Funded Prerequisite)
Before entering the implementation stage, all participating students must complete, or be actively enrolled in, one of the approved preparatory training programmes selected according to their project pathway. The training phase prepares students in:
- Artificial Intelligence (AI) applications for scientific research execution.
- Computational methodologies and digital scientific workflows.
- Introductory Bio-Coding foundations using accessible Low-Code and Zero-Code systems.
- Scientific communication in English and preparation for international research environments.

**Duration & Study Model:**
- Standard Programmes: 3 months | Hybrid Intensive Programme: 5 months
- Full-time model: 5 study days per week, approximately 75 guided training hours.

**Training Fees & Payment Policy:**
- Training Fee: £100 per student.
- Accepted grant participants receive a **25% Training Support Discount** (sponsored by UKABCS and Bioinformatics Gate Egypt), resulting in a net fee of **£75 per student**, payable in 3 flexible installments.
- If the participating university contributes an extra £25 per student from its training budget, the student pays only **£50** (payable in 3 installments).
- All training fees are paid directly to the industrial partner, Bioinformatics Gate Egypt, for educational services. No portion of these fees is paid to or received by UKABCS.

### Phase II — Fully Supported Graduation Project Implementation
Following successful completion of the preparatory phase, students proceed to project implementation.
- **Duration**: 3 months (~75 supervised execution hours).
- **Tuition / Implementation Fees**: **100% Fully Supported — Zero cost to students.**
- **Project Administration & Hosting**: Fully supported at zero cost to students, including cloud server hosting, domain setup, and continuous technical tracking. No project administration or hosting fees apply.

## Equivalent Grant Support Value & Principal Investigator Allowance

Each awarded team of 10 students receives comprehensive in-kind support equivalent to over **£3,250 GBP** per team, alongside a direct cash allowance for the Project Supervisor:

### Project Supervisor Support Grant (Cash Allowance)
Each accepted project nominates one Project Supervisor (Principal Investigator) who receives an annual supervision and management allowance of **£1,200 (One Thousand Two Hundred GBP) per year**. Over the full 3-year grant cycle, this totals **£3,600 GBP** in direct supervisor support.

## Post-Project Startup Opportunity

Graduation project teams that complete the programme may apply for the separate, competitive **[UKABCS Graduation Project Startup Grant](/news/ukabcs-graduation-project-startup-grant)**. Selected projects may receive support to initiate a startup in the UK and **£250–£1,000 in marketing funding per selected project**; selection and funding are not automatic. The startup grant is a separate opportunity, and its application arrangements will be announced separately.
