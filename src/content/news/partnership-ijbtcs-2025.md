London, 25 September 2025 — UKABCS received £220,000 in in-kind waivers and fee reductions from the International Journal of Biotechnology and Computational Science (IJBTCS), intended to reduce barriers to publishing and international conference participation.

## Publication Support Waivers

Support is available only after standard peer-review acceptance. The framework offers full publication-fee waivers for researchers from developing countries and a 50% reduction for researchers from the UK and Europe. UKABCS will issue an official grant letter or credit reference once a paper is accepted.

## Conference Participation Waivers

Researchers may receive up to a 75% registration-fee waiver for IJBTCS international conferences, with a minimum 25% contribution for operational costs. Travel and accommodation are not covered.
