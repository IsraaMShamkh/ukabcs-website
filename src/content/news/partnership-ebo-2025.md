London, 25 September 2025 — UKABCS announces a strategic collaboration with EBO Bio Solution Ltd, including £150,000 in research credits to provide paid services free of charge, enable institutional grants, and expand digital and computational infrastructure.

## SciAnalyzer: Transforming Research with Advanced Analytics

SciAnalyzer supports advanced statistical models, drug-discovery applications, AI-powered interpretation, professional charts and reports, and an accessible interface for computational scientists and non-specialists.

## Shared Vision for the Future of Science

The partnership aims to reduce the gap between large institutions and independent researchers by making advanced computational resources more accessible across global research communities.
