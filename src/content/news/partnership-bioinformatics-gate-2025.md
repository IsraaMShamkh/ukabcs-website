London, 25 September 2025 — Bioinformatics Gate announced a £35,000 in-kind donation to UKABCS, extending world-class training and research opportunity to Arabic-speaking researchers in computational chemistry.

## What the Programme Offers

The Professional Diploma in Computational Chemistry 2026 includes 14 integrated modules, practical experience with industry-standard tools, conference poster opportunities, routes to scientific publication, and certificates accredited in Egypt and the UK.

## Real Research, Real Experience

Participants can work with UK-based research teams, complete a project from idea to publication, and present their work to an international audience. The first round directly benefited 40 Arabic-speaking researchers.
