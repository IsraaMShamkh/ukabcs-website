### Executive Overview & Strategic Mission

The **UK Association for Biotechnology & Computational Science (UKABCS)** establishes transparent, accessible, and high-impact funding and capacity-building pathways for the international scientific community. Modeled after leading international academic bodies such as the UKRI, EMBO, Royal Society, and ISCB, UKABCS supports scientific discovery across computational biology, bioinformatics, drug discovery, translational medicine, and AI-driven biotechnology.

Our **General Research Support Framework** provides tailored support for scientific endeavors that fall outside fixed institutional funding calls. Whether you require high-performance GPU computing allocations, analytical software access, open-access APC publishing waivers, or micro-grant financial support, this dossier outlines the complete **Eligibility Criteria, Member & Beneficiary Benefits, Evaluation Benchmarks, and Application Handoff Protocols**.

---

### Key Beneficiary Categories: Who Can Apply?

UKABCS research support grants and in-kind compute allocations are open to qualified individuals, research laboratories, and institutional consortia across five primary applicant tiers:

#### UKABCS Applicant Tier System & Eligibility Matrix

| Applicant Category | Career & Academic Stage | Key Eligible Support Types |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Senior PIs & Faculty** | Professors, Associate Professors, Lab Heads | Large Consortium Grants, HPC Cluster Allocations |
| **Tier 2: Early Career Researchers (ECR)** | Postdocs, Assistant Professors (<5 yrs post-PhD) | Capacity Building Grants, Seed Compute Credits |
| **Tier 3: Postgraduate Researchers** | Registered PhD & MSc Research Candidates | Dissertation Compute Waivers, Workshop Fellowships |
| **Tier 4: Undergraduate Scholars** | Senior Graduation Project Students | Graduation Project Grants, Training Waivers |
| **Tier 5: Institutional Consortia** | Multi-University & Cross-Border Labs | Joint Strategic Equipment & HPC Node Allocations |

#### Institutional & Regional Eligibility
- **UK Academic Institutions:** Fully eligible for all UKABCS co-funded grants, APC publishing waivers, and regional computational infrastructure support.
- **MENA & International Partner Universities:** Fully eligible for in-kind HPC compute credit allocations, international APC waivers, capacity-building workshops, and joint international research consortia support.
- **Independent & Non-Profit Research Centers:** Eligible upon verification of non-commercial academic research status and adherence to Open Science data-sharing policies.

### Comprehensive Benefit Packages: What Does UKABCS Support Provide?

Awardees of the UKABCS General Research Support Framework receive direct access to our integrated ecosystem of academic grants and industry partner contributions:

#### 1. High-Performance Computing (HPC) & GPU Node Credit Allocations
- **NVIDIA H100 & A100 Tensor Core Compute Credits:** Access to dedicated GPU clusters for deep-learning protein structure prediction (AlphaFold 3, ESMFold, RoseTTAFold-All-Atom) and CUDA-accelerated virtual screening.
- **High-Memory Slurm Compute Nodes:** Multi-socket AMD EPYC nodes with up to 1.5 TB ECC RAM for large-scale metagenomic sequencing assembly and pan-genome mapping.
- **Molecular Dynamics Allocations:** InfiniBand-connected clusters optimized for GROMACS, AMBER, NAMD, and Free Energy Perturbation (FEP) calculations.

#### 2. Open-Access Scientific Publishing & APC Waivers
- **100% APC Article Processing Charge Waivers:** Complete funding coverage for peer-reviewed journal articles published in partner open-access titles such as the *International Journal of Biotechnology & Computational Science (IJBTCS)*.
- **Editorial & Pre-submission Peer Review:** Academic review support to enhance manuscript methodology, statistical rigor, and computational reproducibility prior to journal submission.

#### 3. Professional Training & Skill Capacity Building
- **Full Workshop Waivers:** Complimentary registration for hands-on computational chemistry, molecular docking, NGS data analysis, and AI drug discovery workshops delivered via *Bioinformatics Gate*.
- **Certified Handoff & Mentorship:** Direct pairing with senior computational biologists and biostatisticians for pipeline optimization and methodology verification.

#### 4. Digital Research Tools & Software License Benefits
- **AI Research Reading Platforms:** Subsidized or fully waived access to *GetDocsTalk* for interactive AI document analysis and literature processing.
- **Specialized Research Infrastructure:** Access to *Ebonex Technologies* cloud AI microservices, *HealOra* translational bio-data analytics, and *ClientFlow CRM* grant administration tools.

### Evaluation Criteria & Peer-Review Framework

Every submitted request undergoes a structured, transparent double-blind evaluation by the **UKABCS Technical & Research Review Board** based on four standardized criteria:

| Evaluation Criterion | Weight | Key Assessment Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scientific Merit & Originality** | **30%** | Clarity of research hypothesis, methodological rigor, and scientific contribution |
| **Computational & Technical Feasibility** | **25%** | Hardware justification, pipeline compatibility, and realistic timeline |
| **Capacity Building & Educational Impact** | **25%** | Student training impact, ECR empowerment, and institutional strengthening |
| **Open Science & Data Accessibility** | **20%** | Commitment to open data, public code repositories, and OA publication |

### Step-by-Step Application & Handoff Process

Submitting a general research support request is straightforward and conducted through our centralized portal:

#### Process Handoff Lifecycle

| Step Phase | Action Required | Responsible Party | Expected Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Step 1: Online Intake** | Complete and submit online support request form | Lead Applicant / PI | 15–20 minutes |
| **Step 2: Technical Triage** | Evaluate compute feasibility & pipeline compatibility | UKABCS Steering Board | 3–5 working days |
| **Step 3: Peer-Review Allocation** | Review scientific merit & grant credit approval | Academic Review Panel | 5–7 working days |
| **Step 4: Access Handoff** | Issue HPC tokens, APC waivers & access keys | UKABCS Operations | Within 48 hours of approval |

1. **Prepare Request Summary:** Ensure you have a 300-word executive summary of your research project, target software pipelines, and institutional affiliation details.
2. **Submit Online Form:** Complete the official intake form via the link below.
3. **Technical Assessment:** The UKABCS Secretariat completes technical triage within **5 to 7 working days**.
4. **Notification & Resource Deployment:** Approved applicants receive their compute access keys, credit tokens, or grant award documentation directly via email.
