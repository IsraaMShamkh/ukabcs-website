Through over **£400,000 in donated research credits** and analytical waivers, UKABCS has created an unprecedented bridge between scientific innovators and industrial computing capabilities.

Over the past year, laboratories across four continents have processed complex molecular docking screens and genomic sequence assemblies that would have otherwise been financially prohibitive.

## High-Performance Compute Access for Emerging Universities

High-performance computing (HPC) infrastructure remains one of the largest bottlenecks facing computational biologists in developing regions. UKABCS partnered with leading cloud providers to allocate GPU node clusters specifically tuned for:
- **AlphaFold 3 & RoseTTAFold Structural Modeling**
- **Metagenomic Variant Calling Pipelines**
- **Large-Scale Molecular Dynamics Simulations (GROMACS / NAMD)**

```
Benchmark Metrics (UKABCS Compute Network vs Standard Workstation):
• Genome Alignment (30x WGS): 4.5 hrs vs 72 hrs (16x Faster)
• Virtual Screening (1M Compounds): 8 hrs vs 120 hrs (15x Faster)
```

## Partner Contributions & Transparency

All compute allocations are granted through transparent, peer-reviewed applications. Recipients submit progress summaries to our open research repository, ensuring public accountability and open-access data sharing.

Interested researchers can request compute credits through our [Apply Portal](/programmes-grants) or contact the technical steering group.
