The **UKABCS Graduation Project Startup Grant** is a separate opportunity for promising projects that have completed the [Graduation Project Capacity Building Grants](/news/ukabcs-graduation-project-grant-2026) programme and wish to develop their work into a startup.

## What selected projects may receive

Selected projects may receive support to initiate a startup in the UK and marketing funding of **£250–£1,000 per selected project**. Access to ClientFlow CRM is part of the existing graduation project support package, subject to the programme terms.

## Eligibility and selection

Teams that complete the graduation project programme may apply separately. Applications will be assessed and projects selected competitively; completing the programme does not guarantee selection, company formation or funding.

The application process, selection criteria and dates will be announced separately. For enquiries, contact `programmes-grants@ukabcs.co.uk`.
