In adherence to UK non-profit best practices, the UKABCS Board of Trustees has published its inaugural operational review outlining our strict peer-review criteria, Equality, Diversity & Inclusion (EDI) benchmarks, and financial distributions.

## Core Governance Highlights

1. **100% Non-Profit Financial Model**: Every pound received from public donations, institutional memberships, and philanthropic gifts is dedicated exclusively to research grants, student stipends, and open-access publishing waivers.
2. **Conflict of Interest Safeguards**: Reviewers recuse themselves from evaluating proposals originating from their home institutions or past co-authors.
3. **UKRI Alignment**: Grant evaluation protocols mirror standard UKRI scoring frameworks, measuring scientific excellence, novelty, and societal impact.
