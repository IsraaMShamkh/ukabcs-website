The UK Association for Biotechnology & Computational Science (UKABCS) is pleased to officially open calls for the **2026 Capacity Building Grants Cycle**. This initiative provides peer-reviewed financial and technical resources to empower computational laboratories and research groups worldwide.

## Grant Overview & Objectives

The primary objective of the 2026 Capacity Building Call is to bridge computational resource gaps across developing and established academic institutions. Awards provide direct funding of up to **£15,000 per project**, alongside dedicated high-performance GPU server access and 100% Article Processing Charge (APC) waivers for open-access publications.

### Eligible Scientific Domains
- **Bioinformatics & Structural Biology**: AlphaFold 3 predictions, protein structure refinement, and ligand binding dynamics.
- **Next-Generation Genomics**: Single-cell RNA sequencing analysis, metagenomic assembly, and population genetics pipelines.
- **Chemoinformatics & AI Drug Discovery**: High-throughput virtual screening, machine learning toxicity modeling, and QSAR algorithms.
- **Open-Source Software Development**: R/Python package creation, Nextflow workflows, and reproducible Docker containers.

---

## Application Guidelines & Eligibility Criteria

1. **Host Institution Requirement**: Applicants must hold a research or academic appointment at an accredited UK or international university.
2. **Student Inclusion**: Proposals that include postgraduate or undergraduate student researchers are strongly encouraged and receive positive scoring weighting.
3. **Double-Blind Peer Review**: All submissions undergo double-blind peer review by independent members of the UKABCS Scientific Advisory Board.
4. **Funding Disbursement**: Awarded funds are disbursed directly to host institution research accounts or provided as cloud server compute vouchers.

> "Democratizing access to high-performance computing and transparent peer-reviewed grants is at the heart of the UKABCS mission." — *Prof. Tariq Al-Mansoor, Chair of Trustees*

---

## How to Submit Your Application

Interested research leads should prepare a 4-page proposal dossier including project summary, scientific methodology, milestone timeline, and itemized budget. Proposals are submitted online via the **ClientFlow UKABCS Portal**.

For questions regarding eligibility or partner matching, contact the academic secretariat at `info@ukabcs.co.uk`.
