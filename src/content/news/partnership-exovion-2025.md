London, October 2025 — UKABCS announces a £10,000 in-kind contribution from Exovion, a UK-based leader in scientific and healthcare data analytics. Advanced analytical services and AI-driven research tools will be offered free of charge to UKABCS-affiliated researchers.

## A Gift of Technology, Not Just Funding

The contribution includes AI-assisted data processing and computational modelling, molecular and pharmacokinetic simulation services, multi-omics and clinical-data analysis, visualisation dashboards, and consultation from Exovion data-science specialists.

## Allocation and Impact

The services will support projects selected through the UKABCS Scientific Support Programme 2026, collaborative validation and data-interpretation initiatives, and training in AI and bioinformatics tools. UKABCS will allocate resources transparently on scientific merit and research impact.

## Towards a Sustainable Future for Science

The collaboration aims to expand equitable access to technology and enable researchers to turn data into meaningful discoveries for science and healthcare.
