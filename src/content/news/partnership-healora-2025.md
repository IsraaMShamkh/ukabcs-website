London, October 2025 — HealOra has made a £12,000 in-kind contribution to UKABCS in advanced analytical services and digital healthcare solutions, available free of charge to affiliated researchers and research teams.

## A Unique Form of Support

The donation provides AI-driven biomedical and healthcare data analysis, computational modelling for drug discovery and personalised medicine, multi-omics and clinical dataset interpretation, visualisation dashboards, and advisory support from HealOra’s technical team.

## Empowering Equality in Science

HealOra and UKABCS share the view that access to advanced research tools should not be limited by geography or institutional wealth. Resources will be distributed fairly through scientific-support, collaborative-research, and training programmes.
