## GPU Compute Donation

Ebonex Technologies has made an in-kind donation of **£50,000 in GPU compute** to UKABCS. UKABCS uses this donation to provide researchers and partner universities with subsidised GPU hours and AI research compute. Discounts are set by each university's Compute & AI Access Agreement.

Ebonex Technologies is one of several UKABCS technology partners. Learn more on the [Software & Development](/scientific-software#compute) page.
