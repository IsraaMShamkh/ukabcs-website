UKABCS and **EBO Bio Solution Ltd UK** have signed a landmark agreement providing **£150,000 in advanced computational credits**.

This partnership enables emerging research laboratories in developing nations to run state-of-the-art AlphaFold structural modeling and virtual screening workflows without expensive local server hardware.
