## 1. Official Announcement & Heartfelt Congratulations

The UK Organisation for Biotechnology and Computational Science (UKABCS) extends its highest congratulations and best wishes to Sulaiman Al-Rajhi University — College of Medicine (Basic Sciences Department) in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia on winning the UKABCS Graduation Project Capacity Building & Enhanced Experimental Support Grant 2026 (Application ID GR266-UKABCS-2026).

This international award recognizes the academic distinction of Sulaiman Al-Rajhi University and its commitment to advancing medical education, clinical pharmacology, and bio-computational research.

---

## 2. Student Capacity & Scale of Impact

The international grant provides comprehensive training and experimental support for medical researchers in Saudi Arabia:
- **Awarded Research Teams:** Full capacity support for participating medical graduation project teams at the College of Medicine.
- **Participating Cohort:** Academic support for medical students across the 3-year grant cycle (2026–2029).
- **Scientific Focus:** Clinical Pharmacology, Basic Medical Sciences, AI in Health Data, and Bio-Modeling.

---

## 3. Dual-Phase Capacity Building Framework

Participating medical teams will undertake a specialized scientific journey governed by the UKABCS Dual-Phase Framework:

### Phase I: Capacity Building & Computational Training
- **Full Platform Access:** Provision of credentials to UKABCS digital tools, AI drug analytics, and computational databases.
- **Specialized Training:** Advanced coursework in Clinical Pharmacology Analytics, AI in Medical Research, and Bio-Computational Workflows.

### Phase II: Project Execution & Experimental Validation
- **Wet-Lab Validation:** Practical experimental support to validate medical predictions within university laboratories.
- **Publication Mentorship:** Guidance for drafting and submitting research papers to peer-reviewed international medical journals.

---

## 4. Enhanced Experimental Support & Chemical Cash Fund

To secure essential laboratory materials and medical assay kits, UKABCS has introduced an enhanced experimental funding track:
- **Annual Chemical & Reagents Allocation:** A dedicated cash funding enhancement of £3,000 GBP per year allocated exclusively for purchasing approved Kits & Reagents and laboratory consumables for winning project teams.
- **Total Experimental Funding:** Reaches £9,000 GBP across the 3-year grant cycle.
- **Direct Supplier Disbursement:** Procurement payments are disbursed directly by UKABCS to verified chemical suppliers via Lab Chem Flow.

---

## 5. Main PI Leadership (Dr. Eman Ibrahim Anwar M. Ahmed) & Governance

Dr. Eman Ibrahim Anwar M. Ahmed (Assistant Professor in Clinical Pharmacology) serves as the designated Main Principal Investigator (Main PI). Under Clause 10.1 of the agreement, her responsibilities include:
- **Scientific Review & Specification Approval:** Evaluating and approving technical specifications for Kits & Reagents submitted by medical research teams.
- **Digital Management via Lab Chem Flow:** Overseeing procurement requests, inventory checks, and chemical dispensing through Lab Chem Flow.
- **Resource Consolidation:** Managing shared chemical utilization among project teams to optimize research efficiency.
- **Reporting & Publication Governance:** Submitting Quarterly Progress Reports and Annual Institutional Reports to UKABCS while maintaining institutional attribution for Sulaiman Al-Rajhi University and UKABCS.

## 6. Welcome to the UKABCS Family!

The UK Organisation for Biotechnology and Computational Science (UKABCS) is delighted to officially welcome Sulaiman Al-Rajhi University (College of Medicine) in Saudi Arabia, its faculty, Dr. Eman Ibrahim Anwar, and medical students into the global UKABCS Family.

We are deeply proud of this international collaboration in Saudi Arabia and look forward to celebrating your medical research achievements. Welcome to your UKABCS Family!
