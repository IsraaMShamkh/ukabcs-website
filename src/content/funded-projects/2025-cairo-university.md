The UK Association for Biotechnology & Computational Science (UKABCS) announces that Cairo University (Egypt) has been awarded the UKABCS Capacity Building Grant 2026, as part of an international program with a duration of two and a half years, aimed at enhancing the academic and research capacities of universities worldwide.

This achievement reflects the university’s research excellence and its ability to meet international standards for scientific quality, institutional governance, and sustainable research development.

---

## About UKABCS

The UKABCS is a UK-based non-profit scientific association dedicated to supporting and advancing the fields of biotechnology, computational sciences, computational chemistry, and AI-driven drug design through capacity-building programs targeted at universities, research centers, and scientific societies globally.

The association pursues a long-term strategic vision to foster institutional transformation in research ecosystems, keeping pace with rapid developments in computational and AI sciences.

---

## Vision of the UKABCS Capacity Building Grant Program

The UKABCS Capacity Building Grant program aims to:
- Strengthen institutional academic and research capacity internationally.
- Support cross-border scientific collaboration between universities in the Global North and South.
- Empower researchers across diverse scientific disciplines, beyond purely computational fields.

The program spans two and a half years, and it goes beyond technical training in computational chemistry and drug design. It seeks to embed a new academic culture within universities, where computational methods become an integral part of research across all disciplines.

The program’s vision is built upon:
- Integrating computational approaches as a core element of research.
- Building a sustainable academic environment that supports innovation and accelerates discovery.
- Enhancing international collaboration between universities and researchers.
- Enabling non-profit organizations and scientific societies to benefit from the program.
- Improving the quality of research outputs and positioning institutions as globally competitive.

---

## Program Objectives and Strategic Benefits

This program represents a long-term institutional investment, focusing not only on individual skills but also on building sustainable research capacities within universities. Key objectives and benefits include:
- **Enhancing research quality:** by improving the accuracy of scientific interpretation and analysis.
- **Reducing research costs:** by minimizing reliance on expensive laboratory experiments.
- **Accelerating the research cycle:** from concept to publication.
- **Institutional empowerment:** by embedding computational expertise as a lasting core capacity.
- **Global integration:** enabling researchers to engage with international scientific networks.
- **Building a sustainable academic generation:** equipped with advanced tools for long-term innovation.

---

## Grant Coverage

The program provides full financial coverage for all core educational and research activities, with no additional financial burden on universities or researchers. The UKABCS manages the grant and transfers funds directly to the implementing partners, in line with UKRI standards for transparency and accountability.

### Coverage Includes:
1. A comprehensive academic training program comprising 14 integrated modules.
2. Digital and research infrastructure to support participating teams.
3. Article Processing Charges (APC) for accepted research papers in international journals and conferences.
4. Fees for poster presentations at international conferences.
5. Accredited professional diplomas and certificates for all participants.
