The UKABCS Grants Review Committee has officially awarded the **University of Basrah (Iraq)** the Capacity Building Grant for 2026 following rigorous peer review.

### Research Significance
The research team will investigate microbial genomics in extreme environments, utilizing advanced sequencing data processing tools facilitated by UKABCS research credits.

### Deliverables
- Development of a specialized regional genomic variant database.
- Hands-on workshops in Chemoinformatics and Molecular Dynamics.
