The UK Association for Biotechnology & Computational Science (UKABCS) is pleased to announce that **Cairo University** has been awarded the **UKABCS Capacity Building Grant 2026**.

### Project Scope & Objectives
This grant provides dedicated computational resources, molecular docking software licensing, and academic travel support for researchers at the Faculty of Science and Faculty of Pharmacy. 

### Expected Outcomes
- Training over 45 postgraduate researchers in advanced computational drug discovery.
- Integration with UKABCS cloud infrastructure for large-scale structural bioinformatics pipelines.
- Open-access publication support under UKABCS APC waiver programs.
