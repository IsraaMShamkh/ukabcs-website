## 1. Official Announcement & Heartfelt Congratulations

The UK Organisation for Biotechnology and Computational Science (UKABCS) extends its warmest congratulations to the Center for the Development of Scientific & Educational Connection (Russian Federation) on winning the UKABCS Graduation Project Capacity Building & Enhanced Experimental Support Grant 2026 (Application ID GR265).

This international award celebrates successful cross-border scientific collaboration, advancing computational engineering, data analytics, and biological modeling for participating researchers.

---

## 2. Student Capacity & Scale of Impact

The 2026 grant provides comprehensive training and experimental support for participating researchers in Russia:
- **Awarded Research Teams:** Full capacity support for research and engineering project teams under the Center.
- **Participating Cohort:** Capacity building for researchers across the 3-year grant cycle (2026–2029).
- **Scientific Focus:** Computational Sciences, Bio-Engineering, and Data Analytics.

---

## 3. Dual-Phase Capacity Building Framework

Participating research teams will undertake a specialized scientific journey governed by the UKABCS Dual-Phase Framework:

### Phase I: Capacity Building & Computational Training
- **Full Platform Access:** Provision of credentials to UKABCS digital tools, software pipelines, and databases.
- **Specialized Training:** Advanced coursework in Bio-Computational Modeling, Big Data Analytics, and Engineering Applications.

### Phase II: Project Execution & Experimental Validation
- **Wet-Lab Validation:** Practical experimental support to validate computational predictions within research laboratories.
- **Publication Mentorship:** Academic guidance for drafting and submitting research papers to peer-reviewed international journals.

---

## 4. Enhanced Experimental Support & Chemical Cash Fund

To support experimental implementation and laboratory materials, UKABCS has introduced an enhanced experimental funding track:
- **Annual Chemical & Reagents Allocation:** A dedicated cash funding enhancement of £3,000 GBP per year allocated exclusively for purchasing approved Kits & Reagents and laboratory consumables for winning project teams.
- **Total Experimental Funding:** Reaches £9,000 GBP across the 3-year grant cycle.
- **Direct Supplier Disbursement:** Procurement payments are disbursed directly by UKABCS to verified chemical vendors via Lab Chem Flow.

---

## 5. Main PI & Representative Leadership (Nawras T. Jassim) & Governance

Nawras T. Jassim serves as the designated Main Principal Investigator and Representative for the awarded grant. Under Clause 10.1 of the agreement, responsibilities include:
- **Scientific Review & Specification Approval:** Evaluating and scientifically approving technical specifications for Kits & Reagents submitted by research teams.
- **Digital Management via Lab Chem Flow:** Overseeing procurement requests, inventory checks, and chemical dispensing through Lab Chem Flow.
- **Resource Consolidation:** Managing shared chemical utilization among project teams to maximize research efficiency.
- **Reporting & Publication Governance:** Submitting Quarterly Progress Reports and Annual Institutional Reports to UKABCS while maintaining institutional attribution for the Center and UKABCS.

The UK Organisation for Biotechnology and Computational Science (UKABCS) is thrilled to officially welcome the Center for the Development of Scientific & Educational Connection (Russia), Nawras T. Jassim, and participating researchers into the global UKABCS Family.

We are deeply excited about this international scientific collaboration and look forward to celebrating your research achievements. Welcome to your UKABCS Family!
