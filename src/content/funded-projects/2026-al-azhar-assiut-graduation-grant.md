## 1. Official Announcement & Heartfelt Congratulations

The UK Organisation for Biotechnology and Computational Science (UKABCS) extends its heartfelt congratulations to Al-Azhar University, Assiut Branch (Faculty of Science for Boys) on winning the UKABCS Graduation Project Capacity Building & Enhanced Experimental Support Grant 2026.

This award highlights the outstanding dedication of Al-Azhar University’s Faculty of Science in Assiut towards advancing scientific research, empowering talented students across Upper Egypt, and integrating modern bio-computational methodologies.

---

## 2. Student Capacity & Scale of Impact

The 2026 grant provides comprehensive academic and practical support for undergraduate researchers in Upper Egypt:
- **Awarded Research Teams:** Full capacity support for participating graduation project teams at the Faculty of Science in Assiut.
- **Participating Cohort:** Training and experimental support for undergraduate students across the 3-year grant term (2026–2029).
- **Scientific Focus:** Biological Sciences, Biochemistry, Biotechnology, and Computational Modeling.

---

## 3. Dual-Phase Capacity Building Framework

Participating student teams will undergo a rigorous scientific journey governed by the UKABCS Dual-Phase Framework:

### Phase I: Capacity Building & Computational Training
- **Full Platform Access:** Provision of credentials to UKABCS digital tools, software pipelines, and databases.
- **Specialized Training:** Advanced coursework in Computational Biology, Protein Structural Modeling, and Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Life Sciences.

### Phase II: Project Execution & Experimental Validation
- **Wet-Lab Validation:** Practical experimental support to validate computational predictions in university laboratories.
- **Publication Mentorship:** Guidance for drafting, refining, and submitting high-impact research papers to peer-reviewed international journals.

---

## 4. Enhanced Experimental Support & Chemical Cash Fund

To overcome laboratory material constraints, UKABCS has introduced an enhanced experimental funding track:
- **Annual Chemical & Reagents Allocation:** A dedicated cash funding enhancement of £3,000 GBP per year allocated exclusively for purchasing approved Kits & Reagents and laboratory consumables for winning project teams.
- **Total Experimental Funding:** Reaches £9,000 GBP across the 3-year grant cycle.
- **Direct Supplier Disbursement:** Procurement costs are paid directly by UKABCS to verified chemical vendors via the Lab Chem Flow platform, ensuring hassle-free laboratory delivery.

---

## 5. Main PI Leadership (Prof. Ahmed Salem M. Solieman) & Governance

Prof. Ahmed Salem M. Solieman serves as the designated Main Principal Investigator (Main PI) for the awarded grant. Under Clause 10.1 of the agreement, his responsibilities include:
- **Scientific Review & Specification Approval:** Scientifically reviewing and approving required Kits & Reagents submitted by research supervisors.
- **Digital Management via Lab Chem Flow:** Managing procurement requests, inventory checks, and chemical dispensing through the official Lab Chem Flow digital platform.
- **Resource Consolidation:** Organizing shared chemical utilization among project teams to maximize research and budget efficiency.
- **Reporting & Publication Governance:** Submitting Quarterly Progress Reports and Annual Institutional Reports to UKABCS while maintaining institutional attribution.

## 6. Welcome to the UKABCS Family!

The UK Organisation for Biotechnology and Computational Science (UKABCS) is delighted to officially welcome Al-Azhar University (Assiut Branch), its esteemed faculty, Prof. Ahmed Salem M. Solieman, and brilliant student researchers into the global UKABCS Family.

We are truly honored by this collaboration in Upper Egypt and eagerly anticipate witnessing Al-Azhar's young scientists excel on the global research stage. Welcome to your UKABCS Family!
