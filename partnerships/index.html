Types of Partnerships

How We Can Work Together

Partnerships designed to strengthen research, education, and global scientific collaboration.

↗

Partnership & Donation

Support research, education, and innovation through financial or non-financial contributions that empower researchers and strengthen scientific collaboration.

↗

Academic Partnerships

Collaborate with universities and academic institutions to support researchers and students and co-develop research projects.

↗

Technology Partnerships

Develop and use innovative software and tools with technology companies to facilitate and disseminate research.

↗

Philanthropic Partnerships

Support research activities through donations, sponsorships, and scholarships to maximise scientific and social impact.

↗

Educational Partnerships

Work with schools, training centres, and educational platforms to expand access to knowledge and build student capacity.

↗

Industry Partnerships

Bridge scientific research with industry needs through applied projects and real-world products or solutions.

↗

Service Partnerships

Provide in-kind resources or services, such as lab equipment, software platforms, products, or specialist training.

↗

Other Partnership Models

We welcome innovative partnership ideas. Contact us and submit your proposal for review.

Partnership Process

A Clear Route to Collaboration

From your first enquiry to being listed as an official UKABCS partner.

01

01 · Get in Touch

Submit your interest via the partnership form or email.

02

02 · Initial Review

Our team reviews your proposal to ensure alignment with our mission.

03

03 · Partnership Agreement (MOU)

We prepare a Memorandum of Understanding outlining roles and mutual benefits.

04

04 · Official Approval

The partnership is formally approved within three working days after signing.

05

05 · Listing as a Partner

Your organisation’s name and logo are added to our Partners page and highlighted in our projects.
