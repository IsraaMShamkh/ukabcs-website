Research Dissemination

Publications & Conferences Support

Publishing and presenting research are essential steps in any researcher's journey. Through this programme, UKABCS provides practical support to help researchers publish open-access articles and participate in international conferences, ensuring that their work reaches a wider audience and creates real impact.

Open Access Publishing

Open-Access Article Processing Charge (APC) Waivers

Removing financial barriers to international open-access dissemination.

High article processing charges shouldn't prevent your discoveries from being cited. We cover article processing charges (APCs) in selected high-impact indexed journals, or provide free publication in the official UKABCS journal through an annual institutional support quota, ensuring your computational findings are published Gold Open Access with maximum global reach.

Conference & Travel Aid

International Conference Presentation Grants

Presenting peer-reviewed posters and oral papers at premier symposia.

Present your peer-reviewed posters and oral presentations at premier international conferences. UKABCS provides financial support for researchers to attend and present at international conferences—covering registration fees (50% to 100%), abstract submission expenses, and up to $2,000 USD travel stipends for selected early-career scholars.

Explore Conference Calls →
Official Journal Collaboration

International Journal of Biotechnology & Computational Science (IJBTCS)

£220,000 In-Kind Publication Allocation.

In partnership with the International Journal of Biotechnology & Computational Science (IJBTCS), UKABCS members and grant awardees receive guaranteed fast-track review and full APC waivers under an allocated £220,000 institutional quota, providing an accelerated, peer-reviewed Gold Open Access venue.
